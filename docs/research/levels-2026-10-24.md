# Price-level suite and strategy selector: replay research, 2026-10-24

Raw numbers: `docs/research/levels-2026-10-24.json`. Script: `scripts/run_level_research.py` (runtime ~100 s, 2 threads).
These are **descriptive paper replays** (fill model `conservative_ohlc_v1`, holds capped at 1 day). They are not validated performance estimates, and they are not evidence of edge.

## Gold opening box (GC, MGC): ORB breakout + snapback
- The transcript's 08:20–08:50 NY box **cannot be built from 15m bars**: 08:20 is not on a 15m boundary. Every day is marked `INSUFFICIENT_DATA`, and nothing is guessed. All gold numbers below come from the 08:30–09:00 variant.
  - The feed has only ~5 days of 1m bars and no 5m bars, so the 08:20 box can't be tested historically yet.
- GC, stop at opposite box edge: n=44, mean −0.34R (95% CI −0.70…+0.02), win 25%, PF 0.55, max DD 17.5R.
  - Split by half: first half −0.77R, second half +0.09R. The result is **unstable**.
- Stop alternatives for the snapback, GC (n=9 each, far too small to conclude anything):

| Stop | Mean R |
|---|---|
| box_opposite | −0.68 |
| box_mid | −0.69 |
| retest_extreme | −0.29 |
| two_box_heights (~2 box heights) | −0.01 |

- MGC behaves the same way (same underlying contract).
- Cost ×0 → ×2 moves the mean by only ~±0.02R, because stops are wide relative to cost. Costs are not why this loses.
- Session split: the 5–6 NY-PM entries lost on every trade.

Base-rate Brier (oracle: uses the **test** base rate 0.282) is 0.2025. Every model is slightly **worse** than that constant.

- The ML adds no discrimination on this data. Correctly, it abstains almost always.
- ORB and snapback have too few rows to fit at all (folds 3–4 record `insufficient_rows`).
- **Nothing beats NO_TRADE.** Recommendation: keep every new technique disabled in the registry, and keep the selector in shadow/research mode.

## Limitations
- About 8 weeks of futures/equity history, and n<50 for every gold variant.
- Bars only: no order book or trades, so order-flow confirmation is `missing`.
- No economic calendar.
- The COMEX session ignores the daily maintenance break.
- Many variants were tested with no multiple-testing correction. Treat the best variant per table as optimistic.
